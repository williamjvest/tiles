**Description** [4000]:

> Tiles puts a cinema camera's controls on a page of tiles you lay out yourself, on iPad, iPhone and Mac. Run one
> camera or several from the same page: on the focus puller's cart, in video village, on a car rig or across a field.
>
> EVERY CAMERA ON ONE PAGE
> • Exposure: ISO or EI with the camera's base, shutter angle or speed, white balance presets with Kelvin and tint,
> iris and ND.
> • REC you can trust: a tile shows the camera rolling only once the camera says it is. Link REC, shutter, frame rate
> and white balance across cameras.
> • Monitoring: which LUT and whether it's on, false color, zebra, focus assist, frame guides and clean feed, output
> by output where the camera has outputs.
> • Playback with the same Live and Review switch on every camera, and live view on a tile or full screen.
> • Timecode, take time, battery, media and each camera's latency at a glance.
>
> LAID OUT YOUR WAY
> Drag, resize and add tiles. Portrait and landscape keep their own layouts. A color for each camera, a look of your
> own and a text size of your own.
>
> MADE FOR LONG LINKS
> Over a Wi-Fi HaLow mesh as much as a cable, Tiles sends as little as it can, shows each camera's latency and
> reconnects by itself.
>
> CAMERAS
> Checked on: Sony FX3, FX30, FX5, FX6, BURANO and VENICE 2; Blackmagic PYXIS 12K; ARRI ALEXA Mini.
> Built for, and not yet checked on: Sony FX3A, FX2 and VENICE; Blackmagic URSA Cine, and Blackmagic cameras over
> Bluetooth; ARRI ALEXA Mini LF, ALEXA 35 and AMIRA.
> Try it without a camera: Tiles has demo cameras with every control live.
>
> STREAM DECK AND COMPANION
> A local Control API lets a Stream Deck or Bitfocus Companion drive the same controls.
>
> Tiles never formats, erases or initializes a camera's media.
>
> Tiles is made by Toldwell. It isn't made, endorsed or supported by Sony, Blackmagic Design or ARRI; their names are
> used only to say which cameras Tiles works with.
