Tiles: Camera Control privacy policy

Tiles: Camera Control (Tiles, made by Toldwell) doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

The app talks only to devices on your local network or connected by a cable: the cameras you add, a Stream Deck or Companion controller if you set one up, and, once you set up HaLow, the HaLow radio nodes there, which it finds itself (it asks devices that advertise a web page whether they're one). Camera passwords, the HaLow nodes' password and the Control API token are kept in your device's Keychain. Your camera list and layouts stay on your device, and so does the record of when you agreed to the Terms of Use. Nothing is sent to Toldwell or to anyone else, unless you send us a problem report.

A problem report is sent only when you choose to, from Settings → Report a Problem (on a Mac, Help → Report a Problem). It goes from your own email, with what the report showed you before you sent it: what you wrote, the app's version, your device's model and system version, your cameras' models, addresses and states, and the app's activity log. It never includes a password. We use it only to answer you and fix the problem.
